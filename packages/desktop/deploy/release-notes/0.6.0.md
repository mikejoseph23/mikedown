- AI Activity panel (experimental, off by default): turn on AI features, then the `mikedown.ai.activityPanel` setting, and open **AI Activity…** from the AI menu or the AI tab of Settings to see each AI request of this session with the exact messages sent and the raw response. It is kept in memory only, can be copied or saved to a text file, and is cleared when you turn the setting off.
- Inline suggestions (experimental, off by default): turn on AI features, then **Inline suggestions** on the AI tab of Settings, and dim continuation text appears when you pause while writing. Tab accepts it, Cmd/Ctrl+Right accepts one word, Escape dismisses it, and Cmd/Ctrl+Alt+Space asks on demand. **Local engines only** starts on; with it off, each request sends the text around the cursor to the engine, after the usual consent prompt. Accepted suggestions are listed in AI Activity.
