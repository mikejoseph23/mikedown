# Four-day week pilot

The engineering team ran a six-week pilot of a four-day work week. Output stayed level on shipped features and bug fixes, and sick days dropped by a third. See the [pilot notes](https://example.com/pilot-notes) for the raw numbers.

## What changed

Meetings moved to Tuesday and Wednesday, standups went asynchronous, and Fridays stayed free. Customer response times did not change, because support kept a rotating Friday shift.

- Meetings on Tuesday and Wednesday only
- Async standups in the team channel
- Friday support rotation

## Next steps

The team keeps the schedule for another quarter and reviews it in September. The review script lives in the repo:

```bash
npm run review -- --quarter Q3
```

Open questions go to the planning channel, tagged #planning.
